TechNova Employee Handbook

Working Hours: Employees work 40 hours per week. Core hours are 10 AM to
4 PM.

Leave Policy: Employees receive 20 annual leave days and 12 public
holidays.

Remote Work: Employees may work remotely up to three days per week.

Performance Reviews: Conducted twice each year.

Learning Budget: Every employee receives ₹50,000 annually for
professional development.
