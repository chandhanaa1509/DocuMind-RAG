University Academic Regulations

Attendance: Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in every
course.

Examinations: Mid-semester exams carry 30% weightage. End-semester exams
carry 50% weightage. Assignments and quizzes contribute 20%.

Grading: O: 90–100 A+: 80–89 A: 70–79 B+: 60–69 B: 50–59 RA: Below 50

Internships: Students must complete one internship before graduation.

Library: Books can be borrowed for 14 days. Late fee is ₹5 per day per
book.
