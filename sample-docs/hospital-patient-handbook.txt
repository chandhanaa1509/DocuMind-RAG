CityCare Hospital Patient Handbook

Appointments: Patients should arrive 20 minutes before the scheduled
appointment.

Emergency: Emergency department operates 24/7.

Visiting Hours: 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

Billing: Bills can be paid online or at the billing counter.

Medical Reports: Digital reports are available within 48 hours.

Pharmacy: Hospital pharmacy offers 10% discount for senior citizens.
