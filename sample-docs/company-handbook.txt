DocuMind Labs — Employee Handbook (Sample)

Working Hours
Employees are expected to work 40 hours per week. Core collaboration hours are
10 AM to 4 PM local time. Outside core hours, employees may set their own
schedule as long as deliverables are met on time.

Leave Policy
Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid time off per year, plus 10 public
holidays. Sick leave is separate and uncapped for genuine illness, with a
doctor's note required after 3 consecutive days.

Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require manager approval and are reviewed every 6 months. All remote workers
must be reachable during core collaboration hours.

Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses under $100 can be self-approved and submitted through the
expense portal within 30 days. Expenses above $100 require manager approval
before the purchase is made. Reimbursements are processed within 2 weeks.

Performance Reviews
Performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Reviews are
based on goals set at the start of each cycle, peer feedback, and manager
assessment. Promotions are discussed during the December review cycle only.

Equipment Policy
New employees receive a laptop, monitor, and a one-time $300 home office
stipend within their first month. Equipment is refreshed every 3 years or
sooner if hardware fails.
